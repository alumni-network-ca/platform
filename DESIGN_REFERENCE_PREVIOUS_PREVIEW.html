How it works

Designed to stay accurate, year after year

Alumni own their profile from day one, and a simple twice-yearly check-in keeps it accurate — without relying on Herzliah staff to track everyone down.

Claim, don't fill out

The Alumni Office starts your record from what the school already has. You verify your email and take it from there.

Say what you're open to

Mentoring, referrals, speaking, a coffee chat — set it once, change it anytime. Nothing is visible without your say-so.

A twice-yearly nudge

One click confirms your details are still right, or takes you straight to updating them. That's the whole ask.
