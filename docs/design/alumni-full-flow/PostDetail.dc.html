Class of 2016 reunion recap

Over 80 graduates of the Class of 2016 came back to Herzliah on November 8 for an evening of stories, old yearbooks and a first look at the new science wing.

Ten years later

Graduates travelled from Toronto, New York and Tel Aviv. Many met teachers they hadn't seen since graduation, and several signed up to mentor current students.

  • The full photo album is on the school's website.
  • The Class of 2017 reunion is planned for spring.
"It felt like no time had passed." — a 2016 graduate
